# North Star Principles

> Status: v1.0 — derived directly from the founder's vision (2026-07-07). Stable; changes require founder agreement.

## The North Star Question

> **"Will this help produce safer, more competent, and more confident real-world drivers?"**

If the answer is no, the design is reconsidered — regardless of how impressive it is technically.

## Success Metrics (in priority order)

1. **Real-world driving competence** — measurable skill improvement transferable to actual roads.
2. **Examination success** — first-attempt pass rate of our students vs. national average.
3. **Road safety** — long-term: incident rates of our graduates (the hardest, most valuable proof).
4. Retention, engagement, and revenue — necessary to survive, but instrumental, not terminal.

Software complexity and simulator realism are explicitly **not** success metrics.

## Decision Principles

1. **Education before fidelity.** A feature that improves hazard perception at low fidelity beats one that improves screenshots. Realism is funded only where it changes driver behavior or materially deepens immersion/engagement.
2. **The five questions.** Every feature must answer: Does it improve learning outcomes? Create safer drivers? Improve retention? Create measurable progress? Create business value? Features failing these are cut or deferred, with reasons documented.
3. **Measure or it didn't happen.** Every learning feature ships with its success metric defined ([research/62_EFFICACY_AND_OUTCOMES_MEASUREMENT.md](research/62_EFFICACY_AND_OUTCOMES_MEASUREMENT.md)).
4. **Challenge first implementation ideas.** Research best practices, compare approaches, document alternatives before building ([architecture/07_ARCHITECTURE_DECISION_RECORDS.md](architecture/07_ARCHITECTURE_DECISION_RECORDS.md)).
5. **Ten-year technology horizon.** Prefer boring, durable, well-licensed technology over fashionable technology.
6. **Company, not codebase.** Every feature is also evaluated commercially: acquisition, retention, monetization, B2B/enterprise potential, international expansion.
7. **Long-term learning over short-term scores.** Optimize retention and habit formation, not gameable session scores.
8. **Living blueprint.** When better ideas, educational research, or market evidence appear, we update the specification before implementing.
