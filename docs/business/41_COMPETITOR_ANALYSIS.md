## Global Theory Apps (playbook)

- **Zutobi** (~$4.99/wk sub, 95.8% claimed pass rate): best-in-class 4-step loop — summarized handbook → videos → gamified quizzes → exam simulator. Built as a country-localization machine. **Threat if they localize BG** (unlikely: small ARPU market) — counter is sim + BG-law AI depth. Avoid their weekly-sub pricing.
- **Aceable** (US, $29–165 courses): instructor persona "Ace", bite-size lessons — direct template for our AI tutor personas. Avoid hidden recurring charges (their #1 complaint).
- **Driving Theory Test 4 in 1** (UK, **£5.99 one-off**, 19M learners): cheap one-time + official content + **pass guarantee** (refunds exam fee) dominated a country. Adopt the pass guarantee — cheap CAC weapon.
- **Fahren Lernen Max** (DE) / iTheorie (CH, CHF 19/yr): sold **through driving schools** (B2B2C). Lesson: in school-centric EU markets the school is the channel — Bulgaria's ~2,000 schools are digitally unowned.
- **DrivingTheory4All** (UK, £5.99/yr, "first AI theory app"): its "AI" is adaptive quizzing, **not an LLM tutor. Nobody globally has shipped a true conversational AI driving tutor. The window is open.**

## Driving Simulators

- **OviLex Soft** — ⚠ correction: **Romanian (Cluj-Napoca)**, not Sofia-based as earlier assumed. Driving School Simulator **23M downloads**, Real Driving Sim, bus/truck line; F2P ads+IAP. "Driving school" is game framing — zero real law/curriculum/exam alignment. Proves massive teen demand for the fantasy. Threat LOW-MED. Adopt mission/level structure; avoid ad-soaked F2P feel (destroys parental trust).
- **City Car Driving 2.0** ($29.99 EA, 61% positive): closest to "learn real driving" — rule-violation monitoring, instructor hints, country rule-sets. PC-download only, no Bulgarian rules, no exam link, no browser version. **Feature benchmark, not a BG market competitor.**
- **BeamNG.drive** ($24.99, ~9M copies): physics gold standard, teaches zero law. **CarX Street**: anti-educational. Lesson: **do not compete on physics fidelity — compete on pedagogy + law + exam alignment, where all of these score zero.**

## Professional Sim Vendors (B2B rigs, €2K–€50K+)

Carnetsoft (~€9K software, exited driving-school market), Green Dino (rigs €259–399/mo rental; schools charge ~€25/sim lesson), Virage, Tecknotrove, Simumak, Exail/ECA — all hardware businesses. **Nobody on Earth sells a browser-based B2C educational driving sim. That slot is empty.**

## Regulatory Landscape for Sim Hours (for GTM)

France: **10 of 20 hours** creditable since 2019 (Légifrance). Netherlands: no minimum, free substitution — TU Delft (n=23,000): **sim-trained students pass ~34% more often** ← our evidence-based marketing stat. Germany: reform reportedly in progress [UNVERIFIED]. Spain: complementary only. **Bulgaria: rejected 2019 (4-of-31 proposal), no provision** — position as prep, lobby later with data.

## AI-Tutor UX Benchmarks

Duolingo Max (character tutor with session memory, "Explain My Answer" on every mistake, weekly AI progress re-planning) · Khanmigo (~$44/yr, Socratic, curriculum-guardrailed). **Transfer rules:** tie every AI response to the official question matrix + the student's error history; one-breath explanations (teens don't read essays); Socratic in practice, instant in exam review; persona > chatbot; price AI as the product, not a $30/mo add-on (premium AI features commoditize fast).

## The Five Exploitable Gaps

1. **Zero AI in the Bulgarian market** — every incumbent is static question-drilling; even globally no LLM driving tutor exists.
2. **No browser-based educational sim exists anywhere at consumer prices** — consumer sims teach no law; pro sims cost €2K–50K.
3. **Theory and practice are divorced** — nobody connects a wrong priority-rules answer to practicing that exact junction in a sim. That closed loop is our unique wedge, and the NL 34% stat backs it.
4. **UX generation gap** — incumbents are 2015-era utilities; the audience grew up on Duolingo streaks and OviLex worlds.
5. **The school B2B2C channel is digitally unowned** — Zebra owns it on paper; a white-label "our school includes the AI app + sim" bundle is an easy later sell.

## Pricing Recommendation (B2C anchors)

Context: full course = **BGN 1,343–2,151 (€687–1,100), rising**; parents pay; a failed exam costs a retake fee + weeks of delay.

- **Free tier:** limited listovki + taste of AI tutor and sim (the market expects free questions — don't fight it).
- **Core premium: €9.99–14.99 one-time / 3–4 months** — full AI tutor, question bank, mock exams. **One-time, no auto-renewal** (local payment psychology; 1–2% of course cost).
- **Premium+ "pass-first-time pack": €19.99–24.99** with full simulator + optional pass guarantee (refund retake fee).
- **B2B2C later: €5–8/student wholesale** bundled by schools.
- **Ceiling:** anything smelling like €10+/month subscription fails against free listovki and a €700+ course bill.
