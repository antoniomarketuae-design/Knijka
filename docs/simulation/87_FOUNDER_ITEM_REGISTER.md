# 87 — The founder item register: every finding, atomised, one row each

> **Why this file exists.** The founder played catalog positions 1–50 and wrote up what he saw, by
> hand, in `150 verdict hand written most important.txt` (authoritative) plus two structured
> restatements (`rephrased.txt`, `brief.txt`). Doc 86 deduplicated those symptoms into **58 causes**
> and a fix wave closed 45 of them.
>
> **Deduplication is the right way to FIX and the wrong way to VERIFY.** A cause can be closed while
> the symptom the founder actually saw is still on screen — a marker moved but still wrong for that
> lesson, a sign built but not placed on his map, a fault disarmed but the lesson still hollow. He
> asked for the opposite pass: *"read every word carefully separate each, as individual tasks,
> revise see what is done and what is not, dont blindly believe on the code, go and see with your
> eyes."*
>
> So this register is **one row per thing he said**, in his order, in his words. Nothing merged.
> A row is closed only when someone has **rendered it and looked at it**.

## Verdict vocabulary — use exactly these

| verdict | means |
|---|---|
| `FIXED-SEEN` | Rendered, looked at, the symptom is gone. **A frame path is mandatory.** |
| `BROKEN-SEEN` | Rendered, looked at, the symptom is still there. **A frame path is mandatory.** |
| `PARTIAL-SEEN` | Rendered; improved but the founder would still complain. Frame mandatory. |
| `NOT-A-DEFECT` | Refuted with evidence. Cite the file:line or the pinned test. |
| `UNVERIFIABLE` | Could not be rendered. Say exactly what blocked it — never guess a verdict. |

**`FIXED-CODE-ONLY` is not a verdict.** If you only read the source, the row stays open.

### The gate was RED when this close-out started. Three defects, all introduced by this wave.

It is worth saying plainly, because the whole point of this register is that "fixed" has to mean
something: **the wave shipped red and reported green.** Nine tests were failing across five files,
reproducibly, on two consecutive runs. They were not the memory-pressure worker crashes AGENTS.md
warns about — the same assertions failed with the same numbers both times.

1. **The B73 cut-in fix leaked onto three other scenarios** (`orchestrator/runners.ts`). Three new
   guards — a pacing-speed lift, a six-metre "must be in front" gate, and a "player outran it"
   resolution — were measured against one actor, `FC_CUTTER`, a car merging on a 50 boulevard. They
   were applied to every `CutInLeadCarSpec`. `CutInLeadCarSpec` is also the carrier for
   **`VUCC_CHILD`**: a ten-km/h child on a bicycle in `sc-vu-child-cyclist`, authored
   `maxMatchSpeedMps === cruiseSpeedMps` ("he does not speed up") with `paceAheadM: 400` commented
   *unreachable by design*. Because the child also swerves, the pacing lift took it: **the cap became
   `player + 3 m/s`, so a student doing 30 km/h was chased by a child pedalling at 39.** The drill is
   *hold back behind the child*. Four graded demo verdicts moved. The mirror case was the
   `sc-hz-brake-dont-swerve` escort — `paceAheadM: 1`, **abreast beside your door by design** — whose
   cut could never fire again under a six-metre gate.
   *Fixed here:* all three guards now sit behind `pacesIntoView()` — it changes lane **and** its
   author gave it headroom over its own cruise. `FC_CUTTER` satisfies both, so every number the B73
   lane measured is preserved verbatim; the child and the escort are exempt. Pinned by two new tests
   in `orchestrator/__tests__/cutin-visibility.test.ts`, and the child pin was proved non-vacuous by
   flipping the predicate and watching it fail.
2. **`clipPlan.generated.ts` was stale** against its own freshness gate. Regenerated; the diff is
   eleven `faultTimeSec` values moving 0.1–1.2 s. No clip added or removed.
3. **`content/hazard/items.json` `hz-tram-left-turn`** froze `faultSec 16.55` / `clipStartSec 8.55`
   against a plan that now says 16.45. Updated to 16.45 / 8.45.

**And one defect the second pass found in a fix, which is fixed here too:** B45's new second
pedestrian in `sc-crossing-let-pass` was authored `variant: "elder"`, and `TrafficLayer.tsx:1044`
maps elder → **white cane**. The same file's own rule, twenty lines away, forbids exactly that:
PE-08 *"deliberately stays variant-less: a white cane there would dilute PE-14's unique признак."*
PE-14 is catalog 30 — a whole lesson whose only признак is the cane, on a list where he has already
told us the pedestrian drills repeat each other. The variant is removed.
