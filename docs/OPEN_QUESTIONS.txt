WHAT THIS IS

  Every row below is a defect someone filed against the driving simulator,
  with a photograph of it happening. 1,511 were filed in total; 1,410 have
  been fixed and retired. These 101 are what is left.

  photographed, a judge reads the photograph, and a second pass tries and
  fails to overturn it. That is why 'we fixed it' and 'it is closed' are two
  different things.

  They are grouped by WHAT IS STOPPING EACH ONE, because that is what decides
  who can do something about it.
