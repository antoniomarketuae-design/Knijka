# Product Strategy

> Status: v0.9 draft — 2026-07-07. Will be finalized when competitor and market research (41, 31) returns. Reflects founder decisions: B2C-first, parallel build, 240h sprint to launch.

## Strategy in One Paragraph

Win Bulgarian 17–18-year-olds directly (B2C) with the most engaging, most intelligent way to get a driving license: a Duolingo-grade AI theory academy fused with a browser driving simulator that no theory app has and no sim ties to real learning. Launch fast, charge from day one (freemium), let the AI education brain (knowledge graph + readiness prediction) compound into a moat, and expand later to schools (B2B) and other countries with the same engine.

## Target Customer (initial)

- **Who:** Bulgarian students aged ~17–18 (plus parents as payers), starting or about to start автошкола, native mobile-first users.
- **Their pain:** theory prep is boring (static листовки drilling), car hours are expensive and stressful, failure means paying again; existing apps are utilitarian and dated.
- **Our promise:** learn faster, arrive at the exam *knowing* you're ready (readiness score), and practice real driving decisions before ever touching pedals — for a fraction of one driving lesson's price.

## Positioning

Not "another листовки app" (we out-teach them with AI + adaptivity + sim) and not "a driving game" (we out-teach games with real Bulgarian law and real exam formats). Category of one in Bulgaria: **AI driving academy**.

## Wedge → Expansion

1. **Now (sprint):** B2C theory + sim prototype, Sofia-format everything, freemium premium subscription.
2. **Next:** retention loops (streaks, missions, leaderboards), voice tutor, deeper sim lessons; parents as explicit payer segment.
3. **Then (B2B, opportunistic not dependent):** schools come to us once students ask for it — instructor visibility portal; per-student licensing. We do not *depend* on schools (founder decision) but we accept their money.
4. **Later:** country #2 rule pack (selection criteria in 42), enterprise (fleets/insurers) once efficacy data exists (62).

## Monetization (v1 — grounded in competitor research, doc 41)

**One-time packs, not subscriptions** (Bulgarian teen/parent payment psychology; incumbents anchor at €9.90 one-time; €10+/month would fail against free listovki):

- **Free tier:** limited daily listovki + a taste of the AI tutor and one sim lesson.
- **Core premium: €9.99–14.99 one-time / 3–4 months** — full AI tutor, question bank, mock exams, readiness score.
- **Premium+ "pass-first-time pack": €19.99–24.99** — everything + full simulator; optional pass guarantee (we refund the retake fee — proven CAC weapon from the UK market).
- Later, B2B2C: €5–8/student wholesale via schools.

Market context: full course costs €800–1,200 and rising; ~33% of theory sittings fail (110,467 sittings 2024, ИААА); ~59,600 new licenses/year. Our top price = 1–2% of course cost, cheaper than a single driving lesson, sold against the cost and delay of failing.

## Strategic Principles

- Ship revenue-capable from day one; the market votes with payments, not opinions (58).
- The moat is accumulated: content bank + knowledge graph + per-student twin data improve with every user; graphics don't compound, data does.
- Every sprint feature must serve the 17-year-old's journey to a passed exam; everything else waits (04).
